Politiki y'Ubuzima Bwite

📋 Privacy Policy

Ubuzima bwite bw'abakoresha urubuga rwacu ni ingenzi cyane. Uru rupapuro rusobanura uko dufata amakuru yawe igihe ukoresha Provisoire Rwanda.

01

📊 Amakuru y'abakoresha

Ntabwo dusaba cyangwa ngo tubike amakuru y'ibanga (nk'amazina yuzuye, aderesi, cyangwa nimero za telefone). Amazina yinjizwa mu gihe cy'ikizamini akoreshwa gusa mu kwerekana amanota ku rutonde (leaderboard) kuri mudasobwa yawe.

✅ Ntabwo dukusaba amakuru y'ibanga
02

🍪 Cookies na Analytics

Uru rubuga rukoresha ibimenyetso bya tekiniki (cookies) ndetse na Google Analytics kugira ngo tumenye uko abantu bakoresha urubuga, bitufashe kurushaho kurunononsora no kurukora neza. Ibi ntibituma tumenya umwirondoro wawe bwite.

🔐 Nta makuru bwite akusanywa
03

🛡️ Umutekano

Dukora ibishoboka byose kugira ngo urubuga rube rufite umutekano. Nyamara, nk'uko imbuga zose zimeze, ntitwakwemeza 100% ko nta nzitizi z'ikoranabuhanga zashobora kubaho, ariko twiyemeje kurinda amakuru y'abakoresha yacu.

🛡️ Dukora ibishoboka byose kugira ngo urinde
04

🔄 Guhindura iyi politiki

Dushobora guhindura iyi politiki y'ubuzima bwite igihe cyose bibaye ngombwa. Igihe cyose twakoze impinduka, tuzabigaragaza kuri uru rupapuro.
